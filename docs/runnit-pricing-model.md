# RUNNIT pricing model

Status: proposed operating model for the first public launch.

## Positioning

RUNNIT is a free training ecosystem for committed athletes who are preparing for a race or other goal. The athlete core should remain generous and useful without a paywall: bring existing training data into one place, make the week clearer, and make accountability easier.

RUNNIT earns revenue from optional depth and transactions, not from withholding the basic record of an athlete's work.

## Athlete plans

### RUNNIT Free

Free forever for athletes, including the first 100 launch users. The free plan includes activity recording and imports, connected-device data, the social feed, friends, comments, GIFs, challenges, basic stats and history, races and countdowns, Training Hub plans and folders, calendar, basic strength journaling, achievements, and rewards.

### RUNNIT Pro — planned

Target price: **$8.99/month or $79/year**, subject to validation before billing is activated.

Potential Pro value:

- Adaptive event coaching and race-week guidance.
- Recovery-aware plan adjustments across connected data sources.
- Race predictions, pacing strategy, taper guidance, and deeper event progress.
- Advanced 4–8 week trends and training-load analysis.
- Weekly/monthly reports and exports.
- Deeper route and training analysis.

Pro should remain labeled as planned until these workflows are reliable and differentiated in real use. Do not create a paywall or imply that billing is active before checkout, entitlements, cancellation, and support are ready.

## Coach Marketplace

Coaches can create profiles and service listings for free. RUNNIT charges a **15% commission on completed paid bookings** and does not charge a listing or monthly marketplace fee in the initial model. The marketplace can support discovery, booking, messaging, scheduling, plan delivery, payments, reviews, and coach payouts.

The product should show the commission clearly before a coach accepts the marketplace terms. Refunds, cancellations, disputes, taxes, and payout timing must be visible to both sides and handled consistently.

## Secondary revenue options

These are optional later experiments, not launch dependencies:

- Sponsored challenges and race/event partnerships.
- Affiliate revenue from disclosed gear, apparel, and supplement recommendations.
- Team or club subscriptions with admin and accountability tools.
- Partner-funded rewards and apparel campaigns.

## Guardrails

- Never paywall recording, activity history, core integrations, basic social features, or the ability to train with a race goal.
- Never present planned Pro functionality as available or billable.
- Keep commission, refunds, payout timing, taxes, and platform fees transparent.
- Keep the first 100 athlete launch cohort free and use it to validate retention and willingness to pay.

## Metrics before activating Pro billing

Track engagement with advanced insights, event-training retention, training-block completion, Pro interest-list conversion, coach booking conversion, repeat bookings, marketplace GMV, and commission revenue. Activate Pro billing only when the product can demonstrate recurring value beyond the free training core.
